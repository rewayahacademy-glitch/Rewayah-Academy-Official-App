## English

Statut : revu et perfectionné par l'agent relecteur le 07/11/2026.
Corrections : titre plus naturel (« Teacher's … commitment ») ; « an institution that teaches » →
« an institution for teaching » (fidèle à « institution d'enseignement », évite la répétition) ;

**Teacher's religious, educational and professional commitment**

Rewayah Academy is an institution for teaching the Quran and the values of Islam. It teaches the
Quran and the Sunnah of the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ according to the understanding of his Companions, may
Allah be pleased with them, and of the righteous predecessors (as-salaf as-salih) who followed them
in goodness: this is the way of Ahl as-Sunnah.

The academy and each of its teachers respect and honour:

- the Quran and the authentic Sunnah of the Prophet ﷺ;
- all the Companions of the Prophet ﷺ, and first among them the four rightly guided caliphs: Abu
  Bakr as-Siddiq, Umar ibn al-Khattab, Uthman ibn Affan and Ali ibn Abi Talib, may Allah be pleased
  with them;
- the righteous predecessors (as-salaf as-salih) and their understanding of the religion.

By continuing my application, I confirm that I adhere to this framework. I commit to respecting it in
my teaching, my words, my communications and my conduct, during lessons and in any activity carried
out on behalf of Rewayah Academy, and to making no remark that disparages any of the Companions of
the Prophet ﷺ.

I confirm that the information and statements I have provided as part of my application are sincere
and accurate. I understand that any significant false statement, any serious breach of my
commitments, or any conduct incompatible with this framework or with the academy's educational and
professional obligations may lead to the rejection of my application, the suspension of my
participation or the end of my collaboration, depending on the situation and the applicable rules.

Rewayah Academy reserves the right to take any appropriate action, including legal action as
permitted by applicable law, where verifiable facts justify it, while respecting the rights of each
party.

I confirm that I have understood these requirements and I accept that they constitute a condition of
my application and, where applicable, of my collaboration with Rewayah Academy.

Corán y la Sunna del Profeta Muhammad ﷺ según la comprensión de sus Compañeros, que Allah esté
complacido con ellos, y de los piadosos predecesores (as-salaf as-salih) que los siguieron en el

La academia y cada uno de sus docentes respetan y honran:

- el Corán y la Sunna auténtica del Profeta ﷺ;
- a todos los Compañeros del Profeta ﷺ, y en primer lugar a los cuatro califas bien guiados: Abu
  Bakr as-Siddiq, Umar ibn al-Jattab, Uthman ibn Affan y Ali ibn Abi Talib, que Allah esté complacido
  con ellos;

Confirmo que la información y las declaraciones aportadas en mi candidatura son sinceras y exactas.
Entiendo que toda declaración falsa sustancial, todo incumplimiento grave de mis compromisos o toda
conducta incompatible con este marco o con las obligaciones pedagógicas y profesionales de la
academia puede conllevar el rechazo de mi candidatura, la suspensión de mi participación o el fin de
mi colaboración, según la situación y las normas aplicables.

Rewayah Academy se reserva el derecho de adoptar cualquier medida apropiada, incluido el inicio de
las acciones legales permitidas por la legislación aplicable, cuando hechos verificables lo
justifiquen, respetando los derechos de cada parte.

Confirmo que he comprendido estas exigencias y acepto que constituyen una condición de mi
candidatura y, en su caso, de mi colaboración con Rewayah Academy.
